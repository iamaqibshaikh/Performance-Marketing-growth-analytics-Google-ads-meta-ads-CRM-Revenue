# 📊 GCC Paid Acquisition & CRM Funnel Attribution Audit

An end-to-end multi-channel paid media audit evaluating **6 months of ad spend ($78,000+)** across **Meta Ads (FB/IG)** and **Google Search Ads**, integrated with CRM lead-stage pipelines to identify full-funnel bottlenecks and actual **Closed-Won ROAS**.

---

## 🚀 Executive Business Impact Summary
* **Total Ad Budget Evaluated:** $78,000+ across UAE, KSA, and Kuwait markets.
* **Pipeline Attribution:** Tracked 3,430+ leads through CRM stages (MQL ➔ SQL ➔ Closed-Won).
* **Efficiency Gains:** Reduced overall Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by **18%** and drove peak Google Search campaign **ROAS to 5.42x**.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations & Key Takeaways
1. **Lead Quality vs. Lead Volume:** Meta Ads delivered lower CPL ($15.25), but Google Search Ads delivered **2.7x higher SQL conversion**, making Google Search 50% more cost-effective for enterprise revenue.
2. **Budget Reallocation:** Shifted 25% of top-of-funnel Meta budget into mid-funnel remarketing and high-intent commercial keywords on Google Search.
3. **CAC Optimization:** Discovered B2C lead dilution in high-cost segments; pruned non-converting targeting parameters in Dubai and Riyadh regions.
