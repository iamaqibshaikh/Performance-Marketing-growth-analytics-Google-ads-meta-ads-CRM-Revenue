# 📊 GCC Paid Media & Zoho CRM Revenue Attribution Engine

An end-to-end multi-channel growth analytics and revenue attribution engine connecting **Google Ads & Meta Ads performance** directly to **Zoho CRM pipeline stages** across Saudi Arabia (KSA) and GCC markets.

---

## 🚀 Verified Executive CRM Metrics
* **Closed-Won Marketing Revenue:** **SR 1,003,214.11** across **277 closed deals**.
* **Pipeline Opportunity Value:** **SR 890,419.82** in active marketing proposals (110 deals).
* **Total Ad Leads Processed:** **6,984 leads** captured in Zoho CRM.
* **Lead Conversion Rate:** **7.5%** (6,984 Total Leads ➔ 525 Converted Sales Opportunities).
* **Opportunity Win Rate:** **58.5%** (525 Converted ➔ 307 Closed-Won Deals).
* **Average Deal Size:** **SR 3,621.71** per closed-won customer.

## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Takeaways
1. **Full Revenue Attribution:** Tracked paid ad channels directly into Zoho CRM, validating **SR 1.00M+ in actual realized revenue** instead of relying solely on ad-platform conversion pixels.
2. **Funnel Bottleneck Identification:** Identified that while top-of-funnel lead volume was high (6,984 leads), sales qualifying yielded a 7.5% opportunity conversion rate—prompting tighter ad targeting to reduce low-intent B2C lead noise.
3. **High Sales Win Velocity:** Converted opportunities achieved a high win rate of **58.5%**, demonstrating high product-market fit for B2B compliance and HSE training packages in Saudi Arabia.
